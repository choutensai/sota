\section{Methodology}
\subsection{Formulation}
The system consists of $N$ omnidirectional mobile robots maninupulating an object with polygonal shape whose motion is constrained to a 2D plane. The primary objective is for the robots to cooperatively perform the estimation task to obtain the Center of Mass (COM) of the object in a distributed manner. It is assumed that neither the object nor the robots are subject to kinematic constraints. Each robot $i$ has a circular footprint with radius $r_R$, can measure and exert a normal force $f_i$ within a limited range : $0 < f_i < f_{max}$ at its contact point $C_i$ on the object's surface. It is also assumed that the robots are able to maintain continuous contact with the object and exert an accurately regulated pushing force at its corresponding contact point throughout the manipulation process. The net force and torque that all robots exert on the object in the world-fix frame $W$ are descibe as:

\begin{equation}
F
=
\sum_{i=1}^{N}
\mathbf{F}_{i}
+
F_f
\end{equation}

\subsection{Formation synthesis}

Torque space:

\begin{equation}
\tau_{\max}
=
\max_{0 \leq f \leq f_{\max}}
\; \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{p}_{i}^{\perp T}\mathbf{F}_{i}
\quad
\mathrm{s.t.}
\quad
\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{F}_i = \mathbf{0}.
\end{equation}

\begin{equation}
\tau_{\min}
=
\min_{0 \leq f \leq f_{\max}}
\; \sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{p}_{i}^{\perp T}\mathbf{F}_{i}
\quad
\mathrm{s.t.}
\quad
\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{F}_i = \mathbf{0}.
\end{equation}

Fitness function:

\begin{equation}
J_r
=
\min
\left(
\left|\tau_{\max}-\tau_d\right|,
\left|\tau_{\min}-\tau_d\right|
\right).
\end{equation}

The formation synthesis problem is formulated as:

\begin{equation}
\begin{aligned}
\min_{\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}_{\geq 0}^{N}}
\quad & J(\mathbf{p}) = -J_r(\mathbf{p}) \\
\text{s.t.}\quad
& \exists\,\tilde{\mathbf{F}} \in \mathcal{F}
\quad \text{s.t.}\quad
\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{F}_i = \mathbf{0}, 
\sum_{i=1}^{N} \mathbf{p}_{i}^{\perp T}\mathbf{F}_{i} = \tau_{d}, \\
& \mathrm{frD}(p_i,p_j) \geq d_{\min},
\qquad \forall i \neq j .
\end{aligned}
\end{equation}
