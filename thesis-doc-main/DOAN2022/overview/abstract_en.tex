\begin{abstract}
Knowledge of an object's inertial parameters is essential for effective motion planning and control in robotic manipulation tasks, yet it is commonly assumed to be known in advance. This paper proposes a distributed method for estimating the center of mass (CoM) of a planar polygonal object using a team of omnidirectional mobile robots that interact with the object solely through pushing. The robots are coordinated to generate an excitation trajectory for the object, enabling CoM estimation within a single pushing operation. The problem is decomposed into formation synthesis and force control, which jointly determine the robot contact configurations and pushing forces required to achieve the desired motion. Then, the object's CoM is estimated in a distributed manner using only local kinematic measurements, without requiring acceleration information. Finally, numerical simulations are conducted to validate the theoretical framework. 
\end{abstract}
